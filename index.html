FDE 现在好像被捧上了神坛，火热程度堪比 2021 年留学生圈「文科转码」的热潮。但真正的 AI Native 到底长什么样？这是我从 B站到 AI 创业公司这半年，两个一线场景的完整记录，附可直接复制的 prompt 和模板。

FDE has become the most hyped job title of the year, with the same fever I saw around "humanities majors learning to code" back in 2021. But what does AI-native work actually look like? Here are two first-hand cases from my last six months, moving from Bilibili to an AI startup, with copy-paste prompts and templates included.

THE WHOLE THING IN THREE LINES

  1. 半年前我在 B站 landing 靠 coffee chat 和人传人，今天我在 Noumena landing 靠 AI 读完全部周报纪要。差别不是有没有用 AI，是这家公司的知识写没写下来。
  2. 我一个文科生，两个月独立打通了公司官网的前后端。AI 最被低估的不是它能干活，是它便宜到可以被浪费、可以被无限次否定。
  3. AI 最大的问题不是能力不够，是它不在乎。人的价值在于在乎、审美判断、知道什么时候喊停，以及替 AI 把记忆攒起来。
  1. Six months ago I onboarded at Bilibili through coffee chats and word of mouth. Today I onboard at Noumena by having AI read every weekly report we have ever written. The difference is not whether the company uses AI. It is whether the company writes things down.
  2. I studied humanities. In two months I shipped our company website end to end, front and back. The most underrated thing about AI is not that it can do the work. It is that it is cheap enough to waste and can be rejected a hundred times without cost.
  3. AI's real limitation is not capability. It is that it does not care. Human value now lives in caring, in taste, in knowing when to stop, and in building the memory AI cannot build for itself.

01FDE 是不是旧瓶装新酒？Is FDE Just Old Wine in a New Bottle?

AI 时代的「文科转码」

The "humanities to code" moment of the AI era

这周我去上海跑了两场 FDE 分享，一场飞书办的，一场千问办公办的。听完之后我只有一个感受：大家是不是把 FDE 捧得太高了？

This week I went to two FDE meetups in Shanghai, one hosted by Feishu and one by Qwen Office. I walked out with a single reaction: are we putting FDE on too high a pedestal?

你要说这两场分享真的让我学到了什么叫 AI Native 的写作模式、颠覆性的企业 AI 革命吗？我要打一个大大的问号。

Did either session actually teach me what an AI-native way of working looks like, or show me a genuine enterprise AI revolution? I have serious doubts.

所以今天想聊一个话题：到底什么叫真正的 AI Native？我会从我加入 AI 创业公司之后的一线体感来讲。

So let me ask the real question: what does genuinely AI-native work look like? I will answer it with two things I actually lived through after joining an AI startup.

02场景一 · LandingCase One · Onboarding

同一个人，半年之内，两次入职

Same person. Two onboardings. Six months apart.

半年前我在 B站 landing，新人上手基本靠 coffee chat、靠张嘴问、靠人传人。今年 CNY 推了 100 个直播间，我们开了无数个 3 小时起步的 50 人宣讲会。

Six months ago at Bilibili, getting up to speed meant coffee chats, asking around, and information passed person to person. For the Chinese New Year push we launched 100 live rooms, which meant countless briefing sessions, 50 people each, three hours minimum.

然而今天我加入 Noumena，第一件事是接入飞书 CLI，让 AI 自动读取过往所有周报、周会纪要。

At Noumena, the first thing I did was connect the Feishu CLI and let AI read every weekly report and meeting note the company had ever produced.

关键不在工具。Noumena 从 day one 就保持信息高度透明，所有联创每周向下属汇报工作，所有跨部门协同这周做了什么、进度和失败、甚至大家对 AI 的思考，全部被编织进了巨大的企业 context 里。没有这一层，接什么 CLI 都没用。 The tool is not the point. Noumena has been radically transparent since day one. Every co-founder reports to their team weekly. Every cross-team update, including progress, failures, and half-formed thoughts about AI, gets woven into one enormous company context. Without that layer, no CLI will save you.

可以直接抄走 · 01COPY THIS · 01用 AI 做新人 onboardingOnboard yourself with AI

把公司过往的周报、周会纪要导出来喂给它，然后用这段 prompt：

Export your company's past weekly reports and meeting notes, feed them in, then use this prompt:

ONBOARDING PROMPTONBOARDING PROMPT
这是我们公司过去 N 个月的全部周报和周会纪要。
我的岗位是【职位】，负责【职责范围】。

请基于这些材料告诉我：
1. 我必须掌握的关键信息有哪些，按重要性排序
2. 我应该精读的文档清单，说明每一份为什么重要
3. 我日常需要对接的上下游 POC 是谁，各自负责什么
4. 这家公司有哪些没写在制度里、但大家默认遵守的做事习惯
5. 我这个岗位最容易踩的三个坑是什么

于是我甚至在入职前就蒸馏了我的三位 +1。当我和 COO 说有时候不太理解 CEO 一些比较抽象的需求时，COO 跟我说了一句话，我当时愣了一下。

That is how I had already profiled my three direct managers before my first day. When I told our COO that I sometimes could not follow the CEO's more abstract requests, he said something that stopped me short.

让 CEO 把他的 agent.md 给你，你先和他的 agent 聊，再和他本人聊。

Ask the CEO for his agent.md. Talk to his agent first, then talk to him.

—— 我们 COO，在互联网和 SaaS 行业扎根 20 多年

— our COO, 20+ years in internet and SaaS

为什么这句值得停一下：今天大家总觉得，只有年轻的、天生数字原住民才更 AI Native、更能接受 AI 的玩法。但一个已经在行业里深耕 20 多年的老兵，今天依然能这么彻底地重构自己的工作模式。他不是在「学 AI」，他是用 AI 的逻辑在重新组织沟通方式。AI Native 这件事，真的跟年龄无关。
Why this line matters: we tend to assume only young digital natives can be truly AI-native. But someone with two decades in the industry had rebuilt his own way of working this completely. He was not "learning AI." He was reorganising human communication around how AI works. Being AI-native has nothing to do with age.

可以直接抄走 · 02COPY THIS · 02写一份你自己的 agent.mdWrite your own agent.md

让别人先跟你的 agent 聊，前提是你得有一份。这是最小可用结构：

Before anyone can talk to your agent, you need one. Here is the minimum viable structure:

agent.md
# 关于我的工作方式

## 我最关心什么
（列 3 到 5 条，按优先级。写具体，不要写「质量」这种词）

## 我的判断标准
（一个方案递到我面前，我会先看什么、再看什么）

## 我讨厌的表达方式
（比如：只给结论不给依据 / 一次性抛五个选项让我选）

## 找我之前请先准备
（明确列出你希望对方带着什么来）

## 我常用的术语和它们的确切含义
（这一节最值钱，能省掉大量反复对齐）

更日常的场景是开会。所有会议我都会打开飞书妙记，结束后直接把纪要丢给 AI，它直接帮我改好，不需要任何多余的 prompt。

A more everyday example: meetings. I record everything with Feishu Minutes, then hand the transcript to AI afterwards. It cleans it up without needing any prompt at all.

AI 是并发的、零社交成本的，还能处理巨大的上下文吞吐量。人做不到同时看一年的会议纪要、一次读完五十万字然后回答其中任意一个细节的问题。AI 可以。

AI is parallel, socially frictionless, and handles enormous context throughput. No human can read a year of meeting notes at once, absorb half a million words, and then answer a question about any detail inside them. AI can.

Over the past two months I shipped the biggest project I have ever owned: our company's new website. It went through four major iterations to get here. 04AI 的三个特质Three Properties of AI

除了「能干活」之外，真正改变工作方式的三件事

Beyond "it can do the work," the three things that actually change how you work

01宽度Breadth

AI 能让一个人的覆盖面积突然变大。它什么都不精，但能帮你跑通一个项目的完整闭环。

AI expands the surface area one person can cover. It is not the best at anything, but it can carry an entire project loop end to end.

我的实例：文案、视觉、前端、后端、部署，五个环节以前要五个人，现在一个人能串起来。
My case: copy, visuals, front end, back end, deployment. Five roles before. One person now.

02没有情绪损耗No emotional cost

你跟同事说第三次「不对重来」，关系就裂了。你跟 AI 说 100 次，它下一次还是笑眯眯地给你干。

Tell a colleague "wrong, start over" a third time and something breaks. Tell AI a hundred times and it comes back exactly as willing.

我的实例：调官网动效那两周，我说过的「不对」大概比我这半年跟人说的都多。
My case: during two weeks of tuning site animations I said "not right" more times than I had to humans all year.

03便宜到可以浪费Cheap enough to waste

10 万的外包预算，变成了两个月 Claude 200 刀的 plan，不到 3000 块，外加我个人的时间。

A six-figure RMB agency budget became two months of a $200 Claude plan, under 3,000 RMB, plus my own time.

真正的变化：不是省了钱，是做四个版本再扔掉三个这件事，第一次变得不心疼。
The real shift: not the savings. It is that building four versions and discarding three stopped hurting.
第三条才是真正的分水岭。人的时间贵，所以人做事必须先规划、一次做对。AI 便宜到可以先做三版扔掉两版。功夫从「想清楚」转移到了「快速判断哪版好」。 The third one is the real watershed. Human time is expensive, so humans must plan first and get it right once. AI is cheap enough to build three and throw away two. The effort moves from thinking it through to judging quickly which version is better.

05那人到底干啥？So What Are Humans For?

用了这么久 AI，它最大的问题不是能力不够，是它不在乎

After all this time, AI's biggest problem is not capability. It is that it does not care.

它不在乎

It does not care

它不承担后果，所以它说「小心」的时候，是在模仿小心，不是真的怕。你让它写一封要发给 10 万用户的推送，它写得很快，但它不会多想一秒「这句话发出去会不会出事」。

It bears no consequences, so when it says "be careful" it is imitating caution, not feeling it. Ask it to write a push notification going to 100,000 users and it writes fast, without spending one second on whether that sentence could blow up.

它的审美是平均数

Its taste is an average

做出来的东西你看着觉得「嗯，都对」，但不会有人惊呼「卧槽这个好」。那个天花板，得靠人去顶。

What it produces reads as "yes, all correct." Nobody gasps. That ceiling has to be pushed by a human.

它不会说「这事根本不该做」

It will not tell you to stop

你给了它一个错的方向，它会沿着错的方向跑得非常漂亮，最后交付一个精美的错误。

Point it in the wrong direction and it will run that direction beautifully, delivering an immaculate mistake.

它记不住

It does not remember

你以为它记住了你上个月说的偏好，其实它没有。它的记忆是你一砖一瓦搭起来的，你得替它建，它不会自己长出来。

You think it remembers the preference you stated last month. It does not. Its memory is something you build brick by brick. It will not grow one on its own.

06可以直接抄走的五条Five Questions to Steal

如果你想判断自己公司离 AI Native 有多远

If you want to know how far your company is from AI-native

自查清单

Self-assessment

  • 你公司的关键决策，是写下来的还是开会开出来的？开会的产出是空气，写下来的产出才是 AI 能吃的 context。这一条不过关，接什么模型都没用。
  • 新人 landing 靠人传人，还是靠读系统？如果答案是前者，说明你们的知识还没变成资产。
  • 你的老板有没有一份 agent.md？能把自己的判断标准写成文档的人，才是真的在用 AI 的逻辑组织工作。
  • 你敢不敢做三版然后扔掉两版？不敢，说明你还在用「人的成本」思考问题。
  • AI 的记忆，谁在替它建？没人建，就等于每次都从零开始，所有积累都在蒸发。
  • Are your key decisions written down, or held in meetings?Meeting output is air. Written output is context AI can actually eat. Fail this one and no model will help you.
  • Do new hires onboard through people, or through the system?If it is people, your knowledge has not become an asset yet.
  • Does your manager have an agent.md?Anyone who can write their own judgment criteria into a document is genuinely organising work around how AI works.
  • Would you build three versions and throw away two?If not, you are still pricing work at human cost.
  • Who is building AI's memory?If nobody is, you start from zero every time and everything you learn evaporates.
